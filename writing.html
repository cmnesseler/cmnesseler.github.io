    MiGAZIN, 30 July 2026 (in German)

    A guest article on our field experiment with more than 8,900 sports clubs in Germany: emails signed with Syrian- or Turkish-sounding names received fewer positive replies. I also explain why anti-discrimination campaigns need to be evaluated rigorously.

  • We blurred the gender of soccer players and had people rate their performances − with surprising results

    The Conversation, 8 November 2023

    People rated clips of elite women and men footballers. When they could see the players’ gender, they rated the men higher; when gender was blurred, the difference disappeared.
