Privacy Policy

SportINaer. Last updated: February 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the SportINaer app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was created by Cornel Nesseler (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a free service. It explains how the Application handles your information.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects the following information when you download and use it:

  • Your approximate location
  • The search queries you enter and the number of results each search returns
  • Your device’s operating system and the version of the Application

The Application uses your location to find sports clubs and bus stops near you. Location data is only accessed when you actively use the search functionality. The Application does not track your location in the background.

The Application does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number or login credentials, and you do not need an account to use it.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase Analytics, which collects aggregate usage data to help improve the Application
  • Google Cloud Firestore, which stores search query data for analytics
  • Entur API, which retrieves information about bus stops near the sports clubs

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services.

Data Storage and Retention

Search data (the search term, the number of results, your approximate location and the version of the Application) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and may also be logged locally. This data is used solely for analytics and to improve the Application’s functionality, and it is retained for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this privacy policy. To request deletion of your data, contact the Service Provider at cornel.m.nesseler [at] uis.no.

Location Data

Access to your location is needed for the Application’s core function: finding sports clubs near you. Latitude and longitude may be sent to analytics as part of search logging. You can turn off location access in your device settings; searching for nearby clubs will then be limited.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the Service Provider discovers that a child under 13 has provided personal information, it will be promptly deleted. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at cornel.m.nesseler [at] uis.no.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information and uses commercially acceptable means of safeguarding data, including physical, electronic and procedural safeguards. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data by contacting the Service Provider. You can opt out of location-based data collection by disabling location services for the Application in your device settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time, so you are advised to review this page periodically. Changes are posted on this page and are effective immediately upon posting.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2025-10-16.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact the Service Provider at cornel.m.nesseler [at] uis.no.
