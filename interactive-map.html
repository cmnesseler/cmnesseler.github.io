How to read this widget. Discrimination is the percentage-point gap in positive response rates between German-sounding and foreign-sounding senders (Turkish, Syrian, or Ukrainian), or between male and female senders. Positive values (red) mean the reference group received more positive replies; negative values (blue) mean the reverse. State map: coloring uses German vs. foreign or female vs. male — the breakdowns available at the state level. The gender filter, foreign-origin filter, and combinations modify the figures shown in the right-hand panel at the national level, since cell sizes for these crosses by state are too small to be reliable. Sport view: sport icons are colored by the German-vs-foreign discrimination gap at the national level; the four filters above are not yet applied here, since gender × origin × sport breakdowns are not in the source.
