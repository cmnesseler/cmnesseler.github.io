Using esports as a laboratory

Online games produce large, detailed data. I have used them to study how Russia’s invasion affected Ukrainian and Russian professional players, how players responded when a game character came out as gay, and how obesity relates to performance.

  1. 2024

    Nesseler, C., & Shtrum, V. War and esport. The Russian invasions impact on the performance of Ukrainian and Russian professional players. Games and Culture.

  2. 2022

    Parshakov, P., Naidenova, I., Gomez, C., & Nesseler, C. Do LGBTQ-supportive corporate policies affect consumer behavior? Evidence from the video game industry. Journal of Business Ethics.

  3. 2022

    Parshakov, P., Naidenova, I., Assanskiy, A., & Nesseler, C. Obesity and Individual Performance: The Case of Esports. International Journal of Obesity.
